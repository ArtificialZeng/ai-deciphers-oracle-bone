# Heji-numbered undeciphered oracle graphs — published-literature hunt (2026-11-26)

Task: find undeciphered oracle bone graphs that come with BOTH a verifiable Heji
number AND a quoted inscription (transcription-side candidates, not image-only).
Web search only (browser.search / browser.open). No Heji numbers, frequencies,
inscriptions or readings invented. Guessed readings are never presented as solved.
Closed targets NOT re-proposed: ⟨礻+升⟩ (variant of 升), HUST-OBC X/1850 (variant of 由).

## Verdict

**No candidate fully meets the bar** (verifiable Heji number + complete verbatim
inscription quoted from a scholarly source). Closest is #1 below: Heji number,
glyph description and sentence context all verified from a peer-reviewed paper,
but the full inscription is only partially quotable because the two graphs in
it are images in the PDF and drop out of text extraction.

## Ranked shortlist

### 1. (Closest) Double-臣 graph — Heji 3521 reverse
- **Glyph:** two 臣 stacked vertically (⿱臣臣-like; unencoded, image-only in the paper).
- **Heji:** 《合集》3521 反 (= 《丙編》526 [7]); Period I, diviner Bin 賓 group.
- **Inscription (partial, as printed):** "□入一□" — Takashima: "我們可以把這條卜辭隸定為：[X]入一[Y]。"
  SVO sentence; VO = "進貢一 (Y)" (present one Y as tribute); 入 = 納.
  The target graph is the subject, used as a personal or place name.
  The object graph after 一 is an image in the paper and did not survive text
  extraction, so the inscription cannot be quoted complete from this source.
- **Status:** explicitly an HDC ("historically discontinuous character", reading
  unknown by consensus). Takashima's proposal — read céng/zēng 曾 or zèng 甑 —
  is his own conjecture, NOT established. Do not present as solved.
- **Frequency:** not stated in the paper.
- **Source:** Ken-ichi Takashima 高嶋謙一, "On the Interpretation of [graph]",
  *Bulletin of Chinese Linguistics* 2.2 (2008), pp. 1–4.
  https://www.academia.edu/50176404/On_the_Interpretation_of
- **What it still lacks:** complete verbatim transcription (check 《合集釋文》
  vol. 1 for Heji 3521, or Fudan "四方会同" database, or IHP rubbing DB by
  Heji no. 003521).

### 2. Five-figure glyph — Heji 882 (UNVERIFIED, non-scholarly source)
- **Glyph:** five stylised human-like figures arranged around a central shape.
- **Heji (claimed):** 一期合集八八二 (Heji 882).
- **Inscription (claimed, garbled):** "阴金女癸（贞金癸）" — as captioned on the
  reel; the reading of the caption itself is uncertain and the phrase could not
  be corroborated: web searches for "合集882" and "贞金癸" returned no
  independent confirmation of Heji 882's transcription.
- **Source:** Facebook/Instagram puzzle series "未破解甲骨文" (account 遇見),
  #086, posted 2026-11-08. https://www.facebook.com/reel/1441613127502415/
- **What it lacks:** independent verification of both the Heji number and the
  inscription (check 《合集釋文》 / Sifang Huitong / IHP DB, Heji 000882);
  source is social media, not scholarship. Ranked below #1 for that reason.

### 3. Companion graph (double-臣 + doubled right component) — Heji 37383
- **Glyph:** left half = two 臣 stacked; right half = another component doubled
  vertically; Takashima liding-ises it as a compound of the #1 graph + a
  甑-like component.
- **Heji:** 《續編》3.24.5 / 《合集》37383; Period V, diviner Huang 黃 group.
  Also used as personal/place name.
- **Inscription:** none quoted in the paper text as extracted. Lacks inscription.
- **Source:** same Takashima 2008 paper as #1.

### 4. Single right-component variant — Heji 6536
- **Glyph:** the right-hand component of #3 standing alone (variant form).
- **Heji:** 《合集》6536; Period I, Bin group; used as a proper noun.
- **Inscription:** none quoted in the paper text as extracted. Lacks inscription.
- **Source:** same Takashima 2008 paper as #1. (Also Heji 19095 / 《前編》6.57.6
  is cited in the paper for a single occurrence of a related graph; no
  inscription quoted either.)

### 5. npj GAN "窟" case study — FAILS the bar (listed only to record the check)
- **Glyph:** top–bottom structure, upper component = "person", lower = unknown
  radical B (inferred 穴 "cave/hole"); conjectural reading 窟.
- **Heji / inscription:** none. Main text contains zero occurrences of "Heji";
  the 150 samples come from the Yinqi Wenyuan image platform; conjectures live
  in the supplement "Summary of Conjectural Decipherment Results.pdf" (not
  retrieved; main text gives no Heji/inscription refs for it).
- **Source:** Zeng et al., "Human–computer collaborative approach to the
  decipherment of oracle bone inscriptions with generative adversarial
  networks", *npj Heritage Science* 14:232 (2026), doi:10.1038/s40494-026-02509-4.
  Other conjectural readings in the paper: 笃, 鹭, 篾 — all conjectural, no Heji.

## Leads that produced nothing

- **Lead 1 — Wang Entian 王恩田 (2015), Shandong Provincial Museum 9 fragments:**
  Full text of 〈王獻唐先生征集甲骨文考釋〉 (Fudan Centre online journal,
  2015-08-27) could not be retrieved via web search (not indexed / not found).
  What IS verified, via Wikipedia's "Oracle bone script" article (which cites
  Wang 2015 as ref. 24): the two graphs discussed there are ⟨礻+升⟩,
  inscription "丁未卜，王[礻升]叀父戊？" and ⟨阝+心⟩, inscription
  "戊寅卜，旅貞：王其于[阝心]，亡災？" — both already closed. No other
  undeciphered graph from those 9 fragments surfaced in any published source
  found. Note also: these are Shandong-collected pieces, not Yinxu pieces, so
  they carry no Heji numbers at all (Heji covers Yinxu only).
- **Lead 2 — Wikipedia "spring" glyph (OracleSpring.jpg):** Wikipedia gives no
  Heji number, no transcription and no source for the fragment; it says only
  that the top-left character means "spring" and has no known modern
  descendant. The Commons file page could not be retrieved via search. No
  Heji/inscription found.
- **Lead 3 — Jiang Yubin 蒋玉斌 "酉 + dots", 2,000+ occurrences: NOT
  corroborated.** Repeated searches found only: Jiang's 2018 prize-winning
  decipherment of 蠢, and a 2019 Nankai interview (Zhu Yanmin) noting ~3,000
  undeciphered graphs and that repeatedly occurring undeciphered keywords can
  unlock whole inscription groups. No "酉 + dots" glyph description and no
  2,000+ figure tied to any specific glyph was found in any source. Treat the
  claim as unverified; do not use it.
- **Lead 4 (other lists):** 《甲骨文字综理表》 (Feng Keji / Ning Hongliang,
  eds., 中国文字博物馆, 中西書局, 2024-12, ISBN 9787547523407) lists 2,112
  undeciphered head entries with source tables — the most promising PRINT
  source for further candidates, but its per-entry contents are not available
  on the web; no candidate could be extracted from it. The "未破解甲骨文"
  social-media series' other entries cite non-Heji collections (乙 931,
  存 149, 粹 1575, 辉 142, 屯南 22, 甲 236) and are out of scope for the
  Heji bar. OBSD (Guan et al., ACL 2024): the word "Heji" occurs nowhere in
  the paper; appendix Figs. 10–13 show genuine undeciphered HUST-OBS/EVOBC
  characters with 10 candidate outputs each, identified only by dataset
  image, with no Heji numbers and no inscriptions.
